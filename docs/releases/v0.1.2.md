# MX Mouse v0.1.2 — Reconnect recovery

## Fixed

After reconnecting an MX Master, the helper could fail its initial HID++ handshake and stop trying. Settings still displayed the saved shortcut, but the mouse button did not trigger it.

The helper now retries failed handshakes automatically, backing off from 1 second to 30 seconds between attempts. Retries stop once connected, removed, or shut down. Saved shortcuts are preserved.

## Install

**Apple Silicon · macOS 13+**. Download the ZIP, unzip, and drag MX Mouse Settings into Applications. Quit Settings and stop its helper before replacing an older installation. See [upgrade instructions](https://github.com/ruibeard/logitech-options-plus-alternative/blob/main/docs/setup.md).

Development-signed and **not notarized**; macOS may require Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Use Set Up… to grant helper permissions if requested. Quit competing mouse controllers first.

## Validation and limits

Automated tests cover failed-handshake retry, backoff, timer cleanup, and suppression during normal connection, removal, shutdown, and diagnostic probes. Parser, storage, device, importer, and installer tests pass. The signed app was checked on a connected MX Master 3 for Mac; the user confirmed the shortcut worked after recovery. Repeated physical reconnect and sleep/wake testing remain incomplete.

Device support is unchanged: MX Master 3 for Mac over direct Bluetooth and Logitech M705 through a Unifying receiver. Multiple simultaneous mice remain unsupported.
